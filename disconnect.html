Disconnect from QuickBooks

You can stop Invoice Tool from reaching your QuickBooks Online company at any time, in either of these ways.

From Invoice Tool

  1. Sign in to the Invoice Tool office pages as an administrator.
  2. Open System, then the QuickBooks section.
  3. Choose Disconnect.

From QuickBooks

In QuickBooks Online, open Apps, find Invoice Tool, and disconnect it.

What happens when you disconnect

  • Invoice Tool's access is revoked with Intuit and its stored connection tokens are deleted right away.
  • New jobs are no longer sent to QuickBooks. Invoice Tool keeps working on its own: jobs are reviewed in the office pages and emailed to customers on approval.
  • Invoices, customers and products already created in your QuickBooks company stay there; manage them in QuickBooks.
  • Record matches between Invoice Tool and QuickBooks are kept so a later reconnection to the same company works smoothly. To have them, or any other data, deleted, email us.
