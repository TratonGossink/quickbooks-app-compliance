Invoice Tool by G Force Studio LLC

Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what is needed to turn job details into invoices for the businesses that use Invoice Tool.
  • We never sell personal information, never use it for advertising, and do not use tracking or advertising cookies.
  • QuickBooks data is used only to match records and create invoices at the business's direction. The connection can be removed at any time.
  • Questions or deletion requests: support@gforcstudio.com.

1. Who we are and what this covers

G Force Studio LLC ("G Force Studio", "we", "us"), a Wisconsin limited liability company, provides Invoice Tool, invoicing software used by service businesses (our "Clients"). This policy explains what information Invoice Tool handles, how it is used and shared, and the choices available. It applies to the technician phone app, the office web pages, the emails Invoice Tool sends, and its QuickBooks Online connection.

Each Client decides what information to put into Invoice Tool about its own customers and staff. For that information, G Force Studio acts as the Client's service provider (processor), and the Client is responsible for its own privacy notices to its customers and staff. If you are a Client's customer and have a question about your information, you can contact that business directly or contact us and we will pass your request to them.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: sign people in, receive job details from phones, price and prepare invoices, create PDF and spreadsheet files, send review and invoice emails, and keep an edit history.
  • To create unsent invoices, match customers and items, and attach job photos in the Client's QuickBooks Online company, when the Client has connected it.
  • To produce reports for the Client, such as the weekly summary and pilot report.
  • To keep the Service secure and reliable: preventing misuse, diagnosing errors, monitoring uptime, and taking backups.
  • To support Clients and communicate about the Service.
  • To improve the Service using aggregated, de-identified usage information that does not identify any person or business.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell or rent personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use Client data to train artificial intelligence models.

4. QuickBooks Online data

  • Invoice Tool connects to QuickBooks Online only when a Client administrator chooses to connect it, using Intuit's authorization process. We never see or store QuickBooks passwords.
  • While connected, Invoice Tool reads the Client's customers, products and services, and income accounts to match records, and reads the status of invoices it created. It creates unsent invoices, attaches job photos to them, and adds a customer or product/service to QuickBooks when no matching one exists. It does not send invoices from QuickBooks, record payments, or delete QuickBooks records.
  • QuickBooks data is used only to provide these features for that Client. It is not sold, not shared with other Clients, and not used for advertising.
  • Connection tokens are encrypted when stored. An administrator can disconnect at any time from the office System page; disconnecting revokes the tokens with Intuit and deletes them from Invoice Tool. See the disconnect page. Invoices already created in QuickBooks remain in the Client's QuickBooks company.

5. How information is shared

We share information only as needed to run the Service, with:

  • The Client and its authorized users. Each Client's data is kept separate from other Clients' data.
  • The Client's end customers, when the Client approves an invoice to be emailed to them.
  • Service providers that process information for us under confidentiality and security obligations: cloud hosting (DigitalOcean), file and backup storage (Cloudflare R2), email delivery (Brevo), uptime monitoring (which receives no personal information), and error reporting where enabled.
  • Intuit QuickBooks Online, at the Client's direction when the Client connects it.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights, safety or property of G Force Studio, our Clients or others.
  • Business transfers, to a successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of the business, under terms that protect the information as this policy does.

6. Cookies and on-device storage

Invoice Tool uses only essential cookies to keep users signed in. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The phone app saves job details on the device until they are sent, so work is not lost when there is no signal; this information stays on the device until it is sent or cleared.

7. Security

We protect information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), separate sign-ins for office staff and technicians, hashed PINs, encrypted QuickBooks tokens, a separate database and storage folder for each Client, restricted server access, and encrypted nightly backups. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a security incident affecting personal information, we will notify the affected Client without undue delay and as required by law.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Client data is kept while the Client uses Invoice Tool, so invoices and history remain available.
  • Encrypted backups are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted automatically.
  • When a Client stops using Invoice Tool, we provide an export on request for 30 days and then delete the Client's data from the live system; backups expire within 30 days after that.
  • We may keep limited records longer where the law requires it.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain uses. Because we process most information on behalf of our Clients, we will usually direct your request to the relevant business and help it respond. Clients can correct or remove most information directly in the office pages. To make a request, email support@gforcstudio.com. We will verify the request and respond within the time required by law. We will not discriminate against anyone for exercising these rights.

10. Children

Invoice Tool is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been entered, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Where information is stored

Invoice Tool is operated from the United States, and information is stored in the United States or with our service providers, which may process it in other countries under appropriate safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and, for material changes, notify Client administrators by email or in the Service before the change takes effect.
